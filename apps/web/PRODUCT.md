# AlgoForge product context

AlgoForge is a local-first quantitative research operating system. It turns explicit research missions into preregistered experiments, immutable runs, validation evidence, governed strategies, and deployment simulations. The interface is an inspection and control layer over committed backend records; it is never the source of truth.

## Primary user

The primary user is a technically capable owner-researcher who needs to see what the autonomous system is doing, what evidence it used, what failed, and what remains blocked. Density and traceability matter more than onboarding theatre.

## Product jobs

- Inspect the current research mission and engine state at a glance.
- Trace a claim from research memory through experiment, run, validation, decision, and evidence dossier.
- Start or stop bounded autonomous work without implying private chain-of-thought.
- Distinguish sample, sweep, validation OOS, holdout, and forward evidence.
- Surface missing data or capability as an explicit blocked state, never as a zero.
- Keep the vault as the human-readable evidence mirror while the application database remains canonical.

## Non-negotiables

- No result may be promoted by UI presentation alone.
- Research citations begin as unreviewed evidence.
- The holdout is burn-once and requires matching source and code hashes.
- Monte Carlo deployment simulation and CPCV/CSCV research validation are separate concepts.
- Agents support the research lifecycle; they are not the product's visual centre.
- Motion communicates state changes only and honours reduced-motion preferences.

## Interface character

Desktop-first research control room: warm graphite surfaces, restrained copper selection and focus, semantic green/amber/red, IBM Plex typography, dense tables, strong identifiers, and quiet borders. No glowing orbits, fake thinking animations, marketing hero copy, or decorative particle fields.
