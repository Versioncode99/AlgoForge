# Session structure

Effects tied to the clock: opens, closes, and the transitions between them.

## Economic mechanism

Participation is not uniform across the session. Auction opens and closes concentrate hedging and rebalancing flow that is absent mid-session.

## Templates in this family

_none_

## Data this family needs

`BARS`, `SESSION_CLOCK`
